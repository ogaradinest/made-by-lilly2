There is something quietly reassuring about wrapping yourself in a handmade shawl. Before it reaches your shoulders, it has already travelled through a pair of hands: yarn chosen, colours tested, stitches repeated, loosened and made again. That time is part of the piece. It gives the fabric a softness and personality that a production line cannot copy.

## Every stitch builds on the last

Crochet is especially lovely because every stitch builds on the last one. A small change in tension can create a different drape; a change in yarn can make the same pattern feel airy, cosy or beautifully weighty. The result is never perfectly identical, and that is exactly the charm.

## Made to be worn, not saved

A Made by Lilly shawl is designed to be worn, not saved for "best". Fold it into your everyday coat, throw it over a simple dress, or keep it nearby for a cool garden evening. With gentle care, it becomes more familiar over time: a useful layer, a splash of colour and a reminder that good things do not need to be rushed.
