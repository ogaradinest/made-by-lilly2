An oversized scarf is one of the most useful things you can own. It is longer and narrower than a shawl, which means it wraps, loops and layers in more ways than you might expect.

## 1. The simple drape

Hang it around your neck and let both ends fall straight down the front. Easy, relaxed, and it shows off the whole length of the colour work.

## 2. The single loop

Wrap one end once around your neck and let both ends hang at different lengths. This is the everyday classic over a coat.

## 3. The double loop

Wrap the scarf twice around your neck so it sits close and warm, and tuck the ends in or leave them loose. Best on properly cold days.

## 4. The shoulder wrap

Open the scarf out and wear it across your shoulders like a narrow shawl. It works beautifully over a summer dress on a cool evening.

## 5. The belted layer

Drape it long over a jumper or dress and wear a thin belt over it at the waist. It turns the scarf into part of the outfit rather than an extra.
