Moss stitch, also called linen stitch or granite stitch, makes a flat, close fabric that looks almost woven. It is built from the shortest crochet stitch and single chains, so it lies very flat and does not curl.

## The structure

In UK terms the repeat is: double crochet, chain 1, skip 1 stitch. In US terms the same fabric is single crochet, chain 1, skip 1.

On each following row, the short stitches are worked into the ch-1 spaces of the row below, and the chains sit over the stitches. The offset from row to row creates the tidy, brick-like texture.

## Why it suits colour

Because each row is short, moss stitch is excellent for stripes and colour changes. Colours mix into a tweedy, speckled effect rather than sharp bands, which is lovely with variegated yarns.

## Things to watch

The fabric is denser than most crochet, so it uses more yarn and is warmer. Going up a hook size keeps it soft rather than stiff.
